The product photos in `assets/drinks/` show the brands' own packaging. They were cut out of their white backgrounds and resized for the menu. The trademarks and photos belong to their owners. They are used here only to show the drinks the demo menu sells. Before the site goes live, the restaurant should confirm which brands it serves and that it may use these pictures (most brands supply them to sellers on request).
